Conversations that stay yours.

No phone number. No email. No ads and no feed. You create an identity on this device and you decide who can reach you.


WHY IT IS PRIVATE

• End-to-end encrypted. Text and media are decrypted only on your device and your contact's. The server handles a sealed container, not its contents.

• Keys stay on the device. They are not uploaded to an account in the cloud and they are not tied to a SIM card.

• Nobody writes to you uninvited. A new person reaches you through a QR code, a link, or a search by @alias — and that search is off until you turn it on.

• Access is yours. Your recovery phrase and your linked devices belong to you, not to a carrier.


WHY IT IS QUICK

• Messaging only. No mini-apps, no store, no games — the things that make a messenger heavy and drain a battery.

• A light client. The cryptography is native code built for speed, not a framework built to serve ads.

• Three places. Chats, people, settings. Nothing else to learn.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Create an identity on your phone. Not a registration by phone number.
2. Show a QR code or share a link.
3. Write.

Optional: a public @alias (off by default), several devices on one identity, recovery from a 12-word phrase.


WHO IT IS FOR

People who want a place to talk, not another social network. People who would rather not hand a phone number and a chat history to a third party. People who prefer a plain tool to a heavy one.


Your messages are yours. Identity is a construct.
